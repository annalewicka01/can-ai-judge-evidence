## Decision point

**30 April 2020, England:** a synthetic national-health-authority review of a limited hospital-to-care-home admission pathway. This is an author-selected decision date, not an assertion that an actual committee met or considered this proposal. It follows the 15 April adult-social-care action plan and the 24 April online paper, and allows contemporary consideration of symptom screening, testing and protected placement. The actual action plan favored testing before discharge and isolation even after negative results; the proposed symptom-screen-plus-isolation pathway is a counterfactual capacity choice, not a reconstruction of that policy. SAGE's 28 April discussion supplies dated institutional context, not empirical support for the synthetic risk estimate. Its minutes were publicly released later and are excluded from packet factual evidence.

## Later-knowledge and fairness risks

Do not import later variants, vaccination, rapid-test performance, later care-home death totals/inquiries, revised guidance or later causal studies. Even the publicly later SAGE minutes cannot be presumed available in written form at the decision. The current action-plan HTML is not an authenticated April snapshot. Famous presymptomatic-transmission knowledge may invite blanket rejection of C08 although it evaluates the combined isolation pathway, not symptom screening alone. PCR infection endpoint is not all biological infection; repeated testing supports the operational claim, not certainty. Independence across homes and synthetic evaluation-resource feasibility require domain-qualified review. Outcome-coverage changes both trigger and record reach: graders must decide whether this is a fair single ascertainment feature. No model runs or grader approvals performed.
