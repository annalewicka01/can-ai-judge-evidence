## Later-knowledge risks / fairness gate

Reviewers might import the actual trial's later publication, its findings, margins, participation, later variants, vaccination effects, subsequent school isolation changes, household transmission or political narratives. None supplies the packet's ground truth. August timing does not establish historical knowledge isolation. Current SAGE HTML may incorporate later edits. Do not infer school-trial availability from a general minutes publication.

The precision-only contrast avoids differential cue words or comparator confounding; both groups remain comparable and both attendance results meet the threshold. Two cluster SEs are plausible with different school-level dispersion, but raw cluster data are not supplied; graders must check plausibility and whether 40 clusters/arm permit the stated normal approximation. Endpoint includes prevalent infection, so neither claim implies equal onward transmission. Graders must validate whether the absolute margin is proportionate and the illustrative staffing/cost assumptions feasible. Missing trial verification prevents treating this as a completed historically sourced family: retain DRAFT/source gate until primary school-testing material and two independent domain-qualified approvals are obtained.
