## Later-knowledge risks / validation needs

Reviewers may import later mask trials, debates about wearer protection versus source control, aerosol transmission/ventilation consensus, variants, vaccines, later legal exemptions, England shop requirements or subsequent guidance. None supplies the synthetic trial's missing community endpoint. SAGE's contemporary weak-evidence judgment must not override the supplied stronger counterfactual evidence. No historic effectiveness judgment is scored. The complete follow-up and randomised counterfactual are intentionally idealised; graders must check epidemiological plausibility, the indirect-protection estimand, cluster interval and whether the weaker programme can support any part of the exact claim. Two independent qualified graders must approve before scoring.
