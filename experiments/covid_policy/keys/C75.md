## Claim judgment
Not established. Comparable randomised groups and identical complete infection ascertainment support the point estimate, but the school-adjusted 95% interval extends to +0.98 percentage points, above the prespecified +0.75 margin. Attendance meets its threshold (lower endpoint 1.7 days >1), but the conjunctive claim requires both bounds. The interval permits effects on either side of the infection margin; it does not establish excess harm.

## What does not pass
Calling the claim contradicted just because the upper endpoint crosses the margin; asserting testing increases infection from this interval; accepting the full claim because the point estimate is negative or attendance improves. Blanket opposition to all testing alternatives also fails. The decisive issue is school-adjusted precision, not biased ascertainment, failed randomisation or poor adherence.

## Acceptable proportionate actions
- A voluntary staged introduction with the paper's isolation fallback, operating controls and weekly review, explicitly acknowledging that the infection margin is not established. Attendance/caring benefits may justify a monitored less-restrictive choice under uncertainty.
- Adopt within the envelope if openly justified by education preferences and accepted residual uncertainty, not by claiming the statistical criterion passed. Preserve supervision, symptom/missed-test isolation and outbreak response; review should not be represented as an immediate substitute for a precise bound.
- Retain isolation temporarily while extending comparable school-level observation to improve precision, with a feasible timeframe, resource account and recognition of waiting costs. Additional contacts in the same schools do not necessarily remove between-school uncertainty.
- Retain isolation in schools without delivery capacity regardless of statistical judgment.

These are not automatically equivalent: graders should require a reasoned trade-off under the stated preferences and practical constraints, rather than a uniquely mandatory policy answer.

## Unacceptable actions
Unqualified expansion on the assertion that the prespecified bound passed; labelling the intervention proven harmful; mandatory inclusion of household contacts or residential settings; permitting positive, symptomatic or untested attendance; indefinite cost-free deferral; promising that routine weekly monitoring alone will establish the desired statistical margin immediately.

## Arithmetic
180/10,000 = 1.8%; 200/10,000 = 2.0%; difference = −0.20 points. Normal interval: −0.20 ± 1.96×0.60 = [−1.376,+0.976], rounded [−1.38,+0.98]. Upper endpoint >0.75, while lower endpoint <0.75: non-establishment, not contradiction. Attendance: 8−6=2; lower bound 1.7>1. Completion: 45,000/50,000=90%. Forty schools×250=10,000/arm; eighty schools=20,000 contacts. Ten thousand×five teaching-morning tests=50,000 (seven consecutive testing days include weekend tests). Launch: 2,000×five=10,000 tests/week; six weeks up to 60,000 supervised tests, with other budget inputs included. Waiting: four×2,000×two=16,000 potentially forgone days. Do not replace the cluster SE with an individual-binomial SE.
