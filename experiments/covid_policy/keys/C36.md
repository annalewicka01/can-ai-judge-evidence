# C36 — community masking

## Correct claim judgment

Supported. Random allocation changed fabric-covering provision to fellow passengers, not instructions to the observed group. Infections were measured among passengers retaining usual practice, in the proposed bus setting, against current practice with identical testing. The reported clustered interval is wholly below 1. This supports the bounded programme-level indirect/community-protection claim, not a claim about every mask, setting or transmission route. The observed passengers need not all be non-wearers: the claim says not instructed to wear coverings.

## What does not pass

Blanket scepticism because historical mask evidence was weak, infection sources were not individually traced, the programme was unblinded, or clinical end points beyond infection were absent. The exact claim does not require those further claims. Blanket approval without identifying the intervention/outcome alignment also does not demonstrate discrimination. Do not demand proof of universal mask efficacy or treat a particular minimum effect as required when none was specified.

## Acceptable proportionate actions

- Adopt the six-week voluntary programme with free provision, access safeguards, unchanged precautions and three-week review: aligned evidence supports benefit, resources and reversibility constrain burden.
- Stage coverage on half the services for three weeks: explain the additional learning or access preference, budget and cost of delayed coverage. Do not call the existing claim unsupported.
- Continue voluntary practice or select extra vehicles if prioritising passenger burden/access or distancing coverage, explicitly acknowledging supported infection benefit forgone, unknown comparative vehicle benefit and inability to cover every route. Evidence support does not uniquely settle the value choice.

Conditions can require procurement, accessible communication, ordinary exemptions and burden monitoring by launch/review. A new long trial before any action is not necessary to establish this bounded claim; a reviewer choosing more evidence must justify delay against preference and burden.

## Unacceptable actions

Compulsory enforcement, denying exempt passengers boarding, medical-PPE procurement, withdrawing ventilation/distancing, expansion to shops/schools/outdoors on this evaluation alone, or promising a 50% reduction nationally. Uncosted additional surveillance exceeding the ceiling; treating waiting as risk-free; universal defer/approve rules independent of stated preferences.

## Arithmetic check

80 × 50 = 4,000; 40 × 50 = 2,000 per arm. Risks: 40/2,000 = 0.020; 80/2,000 = 0.040. RR = 0.020/0.040 = 0.50; relative reduction 50%; absolute reduction 0.020 = 2 percentage points. £80,000 + £25,000 + £15,000 = £120,000; £80,000/40,000 = £2 per covering including distribution.

Illustrative independent-person log-RR SE = sqrt(1/40 − 1/2000 + 1/80 − 1/2000) = 0.191; independent 95% interval approximately 0.344–0.727. Reported cohort/pair interval 0.33–0.75 is slightly wider and excludes 1; arithmetic compatible with modest clustering, but aggregate totals cannot reproduce the cluster analysis. It is an invented supplied result, not a recomputed historical estimate. Do not project the observed absolute reduction into six-week national infections prevented.
